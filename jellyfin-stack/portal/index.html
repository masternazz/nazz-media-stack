Your self-hosted media stack

Everything starts here.

Use this page instead of remembering ports or setting up services from the command line. The installer already connects storage, downloads, indexers, subtitles, requests, and the core media libraries.

Shared login: use the admin username and password you chose during installation for Jellyfin, qBittorrent, Profilarr, and Portainer. Optional apps may ask for their own account the first time you open them.

Media automation

Sonarr↗

TV and anime automation with NAS roots, qBittorrent, and Prowlarr preconnected.
Radarr↗

Movie automation with its NAS root, downloader, indexer manager, and Seerr preconnected.
Lidarr↗

Music automation using the same mounted-media and download layout.
Prowlarr↗

Manage indexers once; changes sync to Sonarr, Radarr, and Lidarr.
qBittorrent↗

Downloads through Gluetun, already split into Movies, TV, Anime, Music, Books, and Comics.
Bazarr↗

Subtitle automation, already connected to Sonarr and Radarr.

Books, invites, and statistics

Kavita↗

Read books and comics from the mounted NAS. Create its local admin on first open.
Mylar3↗

Comic management with the same NAS paths available inside the container.
Wizarr↗

Create invitations for your Jellyfin users through a guided web interface.
Jellystat↗

Jellyfin viewing statistics backed by its automatically managed database.

Administration

Portainer↗

Manage containers in a GUI. Your browser may warn about its local self-signed certificate.
Byparr↗

Anti-bot helper used by compatible indexer setups. Most users do not need to touch it.
Gluetun, PostgreSQL, and Recyclarr are backend services and intentionally have no dashboard card. They run automatically. Advanced Recyclarr files remain under /opt/mediastack/config/recyclarr, while Profilarr is the normal web-based TRaSH workflow.
